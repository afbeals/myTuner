Apply shared sticky header/footer and a separately scrolling summary body, keeping safety text and explicit submission. **Accept:** 320/390px and enlarged text retain accessible Close and Send/Retry without scrolling away from them; keyboard focus stays in the active modal and closing restores its trigger.

Admin cannot readily tell which household account/profile or recognizable channel the report concerns. Add permission-safe human labels and concise category/time/status summary; retain exact references in expandable support details. Preserve the exact data disclosure before sending. **Accept:** admin can distinguish two otherwise similar reports without decoding UUIDs; viewer cards reveal only currently permitted context; unavailable references stay neutral.

Consider New/Acknowledged filtering and compact details when actual volume warrants it; retain bounded pagination. **Accept:** an empty viewer/admin understands the next step and status can be scanned without expanding every diagnostic field.
