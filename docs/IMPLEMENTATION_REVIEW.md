## Severity and handling

- **P0:** Immediate critical compromise or broad destructive failure; blocks further delivery.
- **P1:** Serious security, data-integrity or core-use failure that must be corrected before the POC is presented as ready.
- **P2:** Reproducible correctness or usability issue with limited impact or a workaround; record for an owner decision in the second review round.
- **P3:** Lower-impact polish, maintainability or optional coverage improvement; record for an owner decision.

Severity is assigned from the concrete trigger and impact. A hypothetical concern without evidence is not a confirmed finding. Physical acceptance gates remain separate from code defects. After the first round, the owner requested six new reviewers, mandatory fixes for all confirmed P0/P1 findings, and an explicit P2/P3 decision list.
